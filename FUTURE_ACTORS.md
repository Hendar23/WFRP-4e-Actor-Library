# Proposed future actors

This is a backlog, not a list of actors currently in the module. Check rules, installed compendium items and equipment before moving a proposal to `actors/`.

## Orc magic

- Orc Shaman: basic Orc plus the Shaman template in *Tribes and Tribulations*, p. 17. Add the correct casting skills, talents, staff and up to three Petty Magic and three Big Waaagh! spells.
- Orc Great Shaman: basic Orc plus the Shaman Lord template on p. 17. Review the book's spell limits, casting tests and equipment.
- Savage Orc Shaman and Savage Orc Great Shaman: same templates on the Savage Orc profile, with warpaint and Ward 10 while appropriate.

These need support for the book's Waaagh! spell list, its casting and miscast procedures (pp. 94-101), and dependable Foundry spell items. Do not use an ordinary human wizard's lore as a silent substitute. Black Orc shamans are excluded by the book.

## Goblins and related creatures

Review the basic Common, Forest, Night and Gnoblar profiles in *Tribes and Tribulations*, pp. 8-9, and the templates on pp. 16-17. Proposed families: Common Goblin spearman, archer, soldier, elite, boss and wolf rider; Forest Goblin warrior, archer and spider rider; Night Goblin spear carrier, archer, net carrier, boss and fanatic; Gnoblar scavenger and slinger. Add Giant Wolf, Giant Spider and Squig mounts or creatures after verifying their rules. Goblin shamans and Squig specialists need a separate review of spells, mushrooms and other special rules.

## Other specialists

- Skaven poison wind globadier: defer until its distinctive ammunition and attack rules can be implemented faithfully.
- Orc and Goblin war machines and crew: defer until their mechanical attacks and crew requirements can be represented in Foundry.
- Art and roll tables are outside the current scope.
